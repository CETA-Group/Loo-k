How to use this map

Amenities layer: Every real supermarket, convenience store, school, gym, pharmacy, hospital & clinic in the region is plotted as a point. Toggle categories on the right to focus on what matters to you.
Convenience heatmap: Brighter areas have more everyday amenities within walking distance — a quick read on where daily life is easiest without a car.
Click any point to see its name, what's nearby within an ~800 m walk, and open it in Google Maps.
Source: OpenStreetMap via the Overpass API (community-maintained, open data). Rent & cost-of-living metrics are handled separately by the cost-comparison API.
